01 Logo Mark

The Voxema mark is a white circle ring with three concentric arcs wrapping around it — all centred on the same point, each with a small opening in the upper-right quadrant. The circle represents the meeting: a bounded conversation. The concentric arcs are audio ripples propagating outward — voice being captured and understood. The progressive colour fade (white → mid blue → dim blue) reads as distance and depth. The mark is stroke-only: no fills, no gradients on the paths, built for perfect scalability and monochrome rendering as a macOS template image.
