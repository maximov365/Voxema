Mark Concept

The Voxema mark is a V formed from audio equalizer bars. The two converging legs of the V each consist of stacked rectangular bars — recalling an audio spectrum analyzer — that graduate in color from white at the top to electric blue at the vertex.

This encodes two ideas simultaneously: V for Voxema (and "Vox" — Latin for voice) and the visual language of audio capture. The downward-pointing V also suggests convergence: two channels (system audio + microphone) meeting in a single analysis.

Color Rule

Voxema Blue (#0a84ff) is the only blue used in the product. Never mix it with other blue tones. All interactive elements use Voxema Blue as their accent. The Recording Red (#e53e3e) is reserved exclusively for the active recording state and destructive actions — never decorative.

Typeface Rule

Always use -apple-system / SF Pro — no custom fonts. This keeps the app native, fast, and accessible. The only exception is the recording timer which uses ultra-light weight (100) with tabular digits for a precise, instrumental feel. Monospace (SF Mono) is used for timestamps and technical values only.

Voice & Tone

Voxema talks like a precision tool, not a friendly assistant. It doesn't try to be charming — it gets out of the way. Language is concise, specific, and action-oriented. Privacy is stated plainly, not marketed.

✓ We write like this
"Meeting ready"
"Transcription model — whisper-medium"
"36 speakers identified"
"Permanently delete this meeting?"
"Audio never leaves your device."
✗ We don't write like this
"🎉 Awesome! Your meeting summary is ready!"
"We're crunching the numbers for you..."
"Whoops! Something went wrong 😅"
"Are you sure you want to delete this? This cannot be undone!"
"We take your privacy seriously."
✓ Error messages
"Screen Recording permission required. Open System Settings → Privacy."
"Model download failed. Check your connection and try again."
✗ Not like this
"Oops! We couldn't record your meeting. Please try again later!"
"It seems your internet connection might be having issues..."
